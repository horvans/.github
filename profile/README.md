We build software for education businesses in India.

### Horvan Edu Coaching
The all-in-one app for coaching centres:

- **Classes**: batches, attendance, live classes and recordings
- **Tests**: online tests, test series and rank lists
- **Fees**: fee plans, receipts and dues at a glance
- **People**: apps for admins, teachers, students and parents
- Made for low-cost Android phones and slow mobile data, in English and Tamil, with more Indian languages coming

**See it:** [edu.horvans.com](https://edu.horvans.com) - **Company:** [horvans.com](https://horvans.com)
